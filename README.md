## 🌐 Sector Overview & Market Dynamics

The global AI video generation market is estimated at **$4.6 billion to $8.9 billion in 2025–2026** and is accelerating at a rapid Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of **~18% to 34%**. 

The sector exhibits a bifurcated competitive dynamic: it is **highly concentrated at the foundation model & infrastructure tier** (where extreme GPU training costs and frontier multimodal research favor an oligopoly of well-capitalized labs and tech giants like Kuaishou, Runway, Luma, Google DeepMind, and OpenAI), while remaining **heavily fragmented at the application and vertical SaaS layer** (where hundreds of nimble companies compete across niche use cases such as corporate L&D training avatars, e-commerce ad generation, localized dubbing, and programmatic social media video pipelines).
