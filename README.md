# Awesome-AI-Video-Generation-Code-To-Video

## Top AI Video Generation & Code-to-Video Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Programmatic Code-to-Video Engines, Open-Weight Video Diffusion Models, AI Talking-Avatar & Lip-Sync Frameworks & Local GPU Video Pipelines*  

**Last updated: November 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Video Generation & Code-to-Video**. These tools render deterministic MP4/WebM motion graphics and explainer animations directly from React, Python, or TypeScript code, synthesize realistic text/image-to-video clips, generate AI presenter avatars with synchronized speech, and run local diffusion video workflows.



**Examples** include Runway Gen-3 / Gen-4, Pika, Luma Dream Machine, Kling AI, Haiper, Kaiber, Synthesia, HeyGen, Colossyan, DeepBrain AI, and code-video-model/codevideomodel (the category leaders). 



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for programmatic code-to-video frameworks (`CodeVideoModel`, `Remotion`, `Manim`, `Motion Canvas`), open-weight text/image-to-video foundation models (`Wan2.1`, `HunyuanVideo`, `CogVideoX`, `LTX-Video`, `Mochi 1`, `Open-Sora`), and open-source AI avatar/lip-sync engines (`LivePortrait`, `MuseTalk`, `Hallo2`, `SadTalker`) — ideal for developer educators, technical content creators, AI filmmakers, and creative engineers.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Runway (Gen-3 Alpha / Gen-4 & Act-One)](https://runwayml.com)**  

  Industry-leading generative video and AI filmmaking suite offering text/image-to-video, Motion Brush controls, Act-One facial performance capture, and multi-shot video synthesis.



- **[Luma Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai)**  

  High-speed cloud AI video generation platform producing physically coherent, high-dynamic-range clips with start/end keyframe interpolation and camera motion presets.



- **[Kling AI](https://klingai.com)**  

  High-resolution generative video platform supporting long-form 1080p motion synthesis, multi-element consistency, motion brush controls, and character lip-sync.



- **[Pika (Pika Art)](https://pika.art)**  

  Creative AI video and animation platform featuring Pikaffects, Modify Region inpainting, lip-syncing, sound effects, and stylized video generation.



- **[HeyGen](https://heygen.com)**  

  Enterprise AI video and talking-avatar platform offering studio-quality digital twins, instant voice cloning, multi-language video translation with lip-sync, and API automation.



- **[Synthesia](https://synthesia.io)**  

  Cloud AI video communications platform turning text scripts and slide decks into presenter-led training and explainer videos across 140+ languages.



- **[Colossyan](https://colossyan.com)**  

  AI video creation studio tailored for workplace learning and interactive training videos featuring multi-avatar conversations, screen-recording overlays, and auto-translation.



- **[DeepBrain AI (AI Studios)](https://deepbrain.io)**  

  Cloud text-to-video and photorealistic AI avatar platform enabling automated news, educational, and conversational video production via web studio and API.



- **[Haiper & Kaiber](https://haiper.ai)**  

  Creative generative video platforms providing perceptual text/image-to-video synthesis, video repainting, and audio-reactive music-video transformations.



- **[InVideo AI / Argil / Revid AI](https://invideo.io)**  

  Automated prompt-to-video and code/script-to-short-form video platforms combining AI scripting, voiceovers, captions, and stock/generative B-roll assembly.





## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[code-video-model/codevideomodel](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source code-to-video framework and model toolkit engineered to translate programmatic representations, scripts, and structured code instructions into rendered video sequences.



- **[Remotion (`remotion-dev/remotion`)](https://github.com)**  

  Flagship open-source framework for creating programmatic 2D/3D videos, data visualizations, and automated social/explainer clips at scale using React, TypeScript, CSS, and WebGL.



- **[Manim Community (`ManimCommunity/manim`)](https://github.com)**  

  Iconic open-source (`MIT`) Python animation engine (originally created by 3Blue1Brown) for generating precise programmatic mathematical, algorithmic, and technical explainer videos.



- **[Motion Canvas (`motion-canvas/motion-canvas`)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source (`MIT`) TypeScript library and real-time browser timeline editor for authoring code-driven vector animations and synchronized technical video tutorials using generator functions.



- **[Wan2.1 / Wan2.2 (`Wan-Video/Wan2.1`)](https://github.com)**  

  Leading open-source (`Apache-2.0`) video foundation model suite from Alibaba supporting text-to-video, image-to-video, video editing, and visual text generation on consumer and data-center GPUs.



- **[HunyuanVideo (`Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo`)](https://github.com)**  

  Large-scale open-source video generation foundation model delivering cinema-grade motion coherence, realistic physics, and native ComfyUI / Diffusers integration.



- **[CogVideoX (`THUDM/CogVideo`)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source (`Apache-2.0`) Diffusion Transformer video generation family from Tsinghua/Zhipu AI featuring 3D causal VAEs for smooth, temporally consistent text- and image-to-video synthesis.



- **[LTX-Video (`Lightricks/LTX-Video`)](https://github.com)**  

  Real-time open-source DiT-based video generation model capable of synthesizing high-resolution 30 fps clips faster than real time with keyframe and prompt conditioning.



- **[Mochi 1 (`genmoai/mochi`)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source (`Apache-2.0`) Asymmetric Diffusion Transformer (AsymmDiT) video generation model engineered for high-fidelity human motion and detailed prompt adherence.



- **[Open-Sora (`hpcaitech/Open-Sora`)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source (`Apache-2.0`) video generation platform democratizing spatial-temporal Diffusion Transformer training, multi-aspect-ratio rendering, and inference pipelines.



- **[LivePortrait (`KwaiVGI/LivePortrait`)](https://github.com)**  

  Fast, open-source (`MIT`) portrait and talking-head animation engine featuring stitching and retargeting controls for expressive eyes, lips, and head motion from a single image.



- **[MuseTalk (`TMElyralab/MuseTalk`)](https://github.com)**  

  Real-time high-quality open-source (`MIT`) audio-driven lip-sync model capable of 30+ fps facial synchronization on input videos and avatars across multiple languages.



- **[Hallo2 (`fudan-generative-vision/hallo2`) & SadTalker (`OpenTalker/SadTalker`)](https://github.com)**  

  Open-source (`MIT` / `Apache-2.0`) audio-driven portrait animation frameworks generating realistic head pose, facial expressions, and 4K long-duration talking-head videos from audio tracks.



- **[FramePack (`lllyasviel/FramePack`)](https://github.com)**  

  Memory-efficient open-source (`Apache-2.0`) autoregressive next-frame-prediction video diffusion architecture enabling long-horizon 30 fps video generation on 6GB–12GB consumer GPUs.



- **[FFmpeg (`FFmpeg/FFmpeg`) & MoviePy (`Zulko/moviepy`)](https://github.com)**  

  Foundational open-source (`LGPL/GPL` / `MIT`) multimedia processing and Python programmatic video-editing libraries for compositing, subtitling, audio mixing, and batch rendering.



- **[ComfyUI-VideoHelperSuite (`Kosinkadink/ComfyUI-VideoHelperSuite`)](https://github.com)**  

  Essential open-source (`AGPL-3.0`) ComfyUI node toolkit for loading, batching, frame-interpolating, audio-syncing, and encoding high-bitrate MP4/WebM videos locally.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Revideo (`redotvideo/revideo`)](https://github.com)** and **[Liqvid (`liqvidjs/liqvid`)](https://github.com)** for open-source (`MIT`) programmatic TypeScript/React video rendering and interactive web-video authoring.

- **[LatentSync (`bytedance/LatentSync`)](https://github.com)** and **[Wav2Lip (`Rudrabha/Wav2Lip`)](https://github.com)** for audio-conditioned latent diffusion lip-synchronization on real and synthetic video presenters.

- **[Kokoro TTS (`hexgrad/kokoro`)](https://github.com)** and **[F5-TTS (`SWivid/F5-TTS`)](https://github.com)** for fast, natural-sounding open-source voiceovers and zero-shot voice cloning feeding automated code-to-video narrations.

- **[RIFE (`megvii-research/ECCV2022-RIFE`)](https://github.com)** and **[Real-ESRGAN (`xinntao/Real-ESRGAN`)](https://github.com)** for optical-flow 60 fps frame interpolation and super-resolution video upscaling.

- **[MoneyPrinterTurbo (`harry0703/MoneyPrinterTurbo`)](https://github.com)** and **[NarratoAI (`linyqh/NarratoAI`)](https://github.com)** for automated end-to-end Python pipelines combining LLM scripting, TTS voiceover, auto-captions, and FFmpeg video assembly.
